Curricular Practical Training (CPT)

As stated in the U.S. Citizenship & Immigration Services (USCIS) regulations, F-1 students wishing to participate in an off-campus training program that is an integral part of an established curriculum must first obtain work authorization known as CPT. The training is defined as an alternative work/study, internship, cooperative education, or any other type of required internship or practicum which is offered by sponsoring employers through cooperative agreements with the school, and must be in the student's major field of study. You must obtain CPT authorization BEFORE you begin working in any paid or unpaid experience.  Students wishing to engage in a non-required internship experience should review our website on pre-completion OPT.  Any student wishing to pursue an internship or work experience in their field of study but not required for degree completion will need to apply to USCIS and use pre-completion OPT as a way to receive work authorization. 
 

Processing time alert: Although processing time in iTerp for CPT can take between 3-5 business days, remember that you will be working with your employer and any course enrollment requirements

We recommend you start on this process as early as possible but no later than two weeks prior to your anticipated start date.

September 2, 2026


The U.S. Department of Homeland Security’s (DHS) Student & Exchange Visitor Program (SEVP) issued a Broadcast Message on August 24, 2026 that provided all Designated School Officials (DSOs) with guidance regarding Curricular Practical Training (CPT) policy and clarified their earlier CPT guidance issued on August 12, which stated that CPT must be “a core and essential part of the student’s studies, without which, they would be unable to complete their degree”.

These messages communicate a narrower interpretation of when CPT may be considered an “integral part” of an established curriculum. Among other points, SEVP indicates that CPT should only be authorized when the practical training is required for all students in the program’s curriculum and the degree or program cannot be completed without it.

We recognize that this new CPT guidance may raise questions for students and academic departments. International Student & Scholar Services (ISSS) is carefully reviewing UMD’s CPT policy in light of these messages to assess the implications for our current CPT practices. We are working to analyze and interpret this new guidance to ensure F-1 students’ and UMD’s compliance with federal regulations.

While this work takes place, effective immediately, ISSS is pausing authorization of any unapproved CPT requests that are not required practical training experiences for all students in an academic degree program, required course or required for thesis or dissertation research.

Although students may continue to submit CPT requests through iTerp, ISSS cannot accept CPT requests for UNIV099, ENCO099, BMGT099 and any other elective internships.

Students who already have authorized CPT should continue to follow the employer, location, dates, and other conditions listed on their current Form I-20. Students with pending  iTerp CPT requests should work with their employers to inform them of the pause.  

ISSS understands the uncertainty this temporary pause causes. Please continue to monitor the ISSS website and university email for updates.
 

Eligibility for CPT

  • You must have been maintaining F-1 status for at least:
    • Semester students: one academic year (2 semesters)
    • 12 Week Term students: 4 terms
  • Be a student in good academic standing
  • Internship/training/research must be a requirement for the degree or dissertation completion
  • GPA requirements:
    • Undergraduates: 2.00
    • Graduates: 3.00
  • Students who change degree programs must complete one semester in the new program and receive a qualifying GPA before applying for CPT.
    • This includes; transfer students, Bachelors students who graduate and begin a graduate program and UMCP Masters students who begin a UMD PhD program.

Limitations

  • Hours per week:
    • No more than 20 hours per week during the semester, including on campus work (part-time)
    • No maximum limit during school vacations (full-time)
    • Only part-time CPT in student's final term (including summer)
  • Location:
    • Students are limited to CPT in the DC metro area during Fall/Spring semesters in order to fulfill their in-person study requirements.
    • Students must be physically present in the US during the CPT experience. CPT cannot be authorized for remote work that the student will conduct from outside the US.
    • CPT can only be authorized for employers that have a physical US location (no P.O. Boxes)
  • CPT's effect on Optional Practical Training (OPT)
    • Students who participate in 11+ months of full-time CPT become ineligible to apply for OPT
  • Thesis/dissertation students in final term
    • PhD students in final semester: CPT must end no later than two weeks prior to the scheduled dissertation defense date, in accordance with the Graduate School’s submission requirements. If CPT was originally authorized beyond the permitted end date, the authorization period will be shortened accordingly.
    • Master’s thesis students in final semester: CPT cannot be authorized beyond the thesis defense date. If CPT was originally authorized past the defense date, the authorization period will be shortened accordingly.
  • Deadlines and Changes
    • CPT authorizations are limited to once per semester; please consider your placements carefully before applying for your authorization.
    • We recommend discussing the total length of time that you need to complete the internship project and using the latest possible estimated end date on your offer letter documentation.
    • Please submit your requests to sign up for the course associated with CPT 5 days prior to the ISSS submission deadline. Your CPT must start on or before the deadline.

Application Procedure

  • Obtain an internship/co-op offer letter from an employer which includes all of the following information:
    • Official company letterhead
    • An authorized signature
    • Specific start and end dates of the internship/co-op*
    • Number of hours per week
    • Address of the workplace (if your employer address is different from the work site, please provide both addresses. No PO Box addresses)
    • Amount of compensation
    • Job description (specific job responsibilities)
    • The name of your supervisor during the internship/co-op
    • The specific term internship or co-op
    • If you are a Masters thesis/Ph.D. student in your research phase: a statement that the employer understands that this work will be incorporated as  part of your thesis/dissertation research.

*Internship dates must be within the semester you are registered for the course associated with your CPT (see Step 2).You can find semester dates for each academic year here. If your internship start date has passed or will pass by the time ISSS reviews your CPT application, please include an email from your employer indicating a new later start date.

Click here to see an example of an internship/co-op offer letter.

Students are urged to review the CPT checklist to confirm all required documents are uploaded and all necessary details are included in the offer letter. Any student working remotely on CPT must have this confirmed in the offer letter or in supporting documentation, the address where you will work remotely must also be mentioned by the employer.

Register for an academic component (course) that is required for the completion of your degree.

PLEASE register for the academic component a minimum of 5 days prior to the CPT start date. It can take a week to get approval to add the course.

Below are descriptions of the different options and required supporting documentation:

  • Required Internship course-use when internship is required to meet degree requirements.
    • Provide copy of degree program requirements documenting required internship.
  • Research credits (799,898, or 899): When research/training is required off campus to complete the dissertation the advisor must write a letter to substantiate the need on why it's required for the completion of the dissertation. 
    • Letter of support from academic advisor is needed. It must contain the following information:
      • The subject of the thesis/dissertation & the relationship to the work
        • ​​Please include specific details about what elements of the internship will be required for the completion of the dissertation. Examples could include, but are not limited to:
          • Will the student have access to data during this internship that will be an essential part of the dissertation?
          • Will the student be working with specific research methods or data collection methods that form an essential part of the dissertation?
          • Will the experiences from this internship be incorporated into specific chapter(s) of the student’s dissertation?
      • Explanation as to why this student would not be able to gain a similar experience from training sources available on campus
      • Recommended period of training & number of hours per week
      • Statement confirming that “this training experience is absolutely necessary for the completion of the thesis/dissertation”.​
      • The support letter from the advisor must be on UMD letterhead and include an authorized signature.

Elective Internship courses such as UNIV099/ENCO099/BMGT can no longer be used for CPT course registration. Students must use courses within their department that are required for the completion of the degree.

If you are pursuing CPT in an optional term (Summer, Winter), please register for a full course of study in the required term immediately following your internship (Fall, Spring) before submitting your CPT authorization. If you do not require a full course of study to complete the program in that next required term, please register for all required courses and submit a request for part-time authorization on iTerp.

Submit a request in iTerp to obtain CPT authorization. Upload documentation and provide information in the CPT Request eform in iTerp, found under F-1 Student Services. ISSS processes all requests for CPT as quickly as possible and typically within 3-5 business days. Requests are processed in the order they are received so as not to disadvantage other students who are in a similar rush to receive their authorization. Please ensure that all information is complete!

 If it is approved, you will receive a new I-20 with your employment authorization printed on the second page. You do not have the authorization to begin working until you receive this I-20 showing the employment authorization on the second page.

Need more information? ISSS offers a workshop for F-1 international students on the process of applying for CPT. Please note that your attendance at this workshop is not required in order to apply for CPT. Check the International Terp Career Development series for upcoming dates.

Special Situations Affecting Approval of CPT

CPT authorizations typically require one course registration per term in which the student is engaging in the internship. Most student situations will require a separate winter term registration if they are engaging in an internship over winter term.  

However, due to the condensed nature of the winter term, ISSS will agree to waive the requirement for student to register for a winter term internship course if they meet all of the following criteria:

  • Student will be engaging in training/research for the same employer beginning in the Fall and continuing through Winter, or beginning in Winter and continuing through the duration of the Spring;
  • Student will be keeping the same number of hours per week for the duration of the internship; and
  • At the time of the CPT authorization application, student’s offer letter specifies the full start and end dates for the duration of the internship, including the employment that will occur during winter term.
  • Students that do not meet all three of these criteria should plan on registering for a winter term internship course in order to be authorized for CPT during the winter.

During the semester, F-1 students may not exceed a combined total of 20 hours of employment authorization per week. This combined total includes both on-campus and off-campus employment, such as CPT. If you are reducing the number of hours per week you will work or completely terminating your on-campus employment in order to participate in a CPT experience, we will need a letter on official University letterhead from your department/HR that contains the following information:

Hourly on-campus work

Graduate Assistantships

Reduction of hours only
  • Effective start date for the reduction of hourly work
  • New number of hours per week
  • Effective start date for the reduction in hours 
  • New number of hours per week 
  • Effective start date for the reduction of benefits associated with assistantship (tuition remission, health benefits) 
  • The new stipend amount that student will receive through the duration of the assistantship

Termination of on-campus employment
  • Effective date of termination of on-campus employment
  • Effective date of termination of graduate assistantship 
  • Effective date of termination of benefits associated with assistantship (tuition remission, health benefits)

Graduate students who are reducing or terminating an assistantship will  be required to show proof of liquid finances for up to one calendar year in order to update the estimated expenses on the I-20.  Please view acceptable sources of funding here.

Special Requests for Full-time CPT During the Semester for Required Research Experiences (799/898/899 only)

The F-1 regulations specify that all F-1 students, whether or not they are engaged in thesis/dissertation research, are restricted to engaging in part-time CPT only (20 hours per week or less) while school is in session. The ISSS office will only consider an exception to permit full-time CPT authorization on a case-by-case basis for students engaging in a required research experience (799/898/899).  Circumstances must be truly exceptional to prohibit the student from engaging in part-time work during the internship.

In addition to the required application information, students asking for an exception to the part-time restriction during the semester should prepare the documentation listed below and submit it with all other CPT documentation at the time of application:

  • Statement from the internship employer and academic advisor (to the best of their knowledge) explaining the exceptional circumstances that would prevent the student from engaging in part-time employment (20 hours per week or less) during the semester.
  • For students who currently hold an assistantship or on-campus employment only: You will not be permitted to be employed on campus concurrently if you are requesting an exception to participate in full-time CPT during the semester. If you currently have an assistantship or on-campus employment, please submit:
    • A letter from your department/HR detailing the termination of your on-campus employment (see chart in “Ending on-campus employment to participate in CPT” above); and
    • Proof of liquid finances for up to one calendar year (see “Ending on-campus employment to participate in CPT” above)

NOTE: Exceptions are considered on a case-by-case basis, and having all of the above documentation does not in any way guarantee that the student will be approved for the exception.  Please allow 1-2 weeks for the ISSS staff to consider your request for an exception.
